About

table.annotations marks rows, columns and cells as errors, warnings or info: each gets a tint, a popover lists the annotations where you hover, grouped by scope, and F8 takes the cursor from one to the next.

  1. Click a scenario (A–E) to add a specific set of annotations.
  2. Hover an annotated cell or header to open the popover.
  3. Click in the table and press F8 or Shift + F8 (Alt + ↓ or ↑) to go to the next or previous one.
  4. Add 100,000 annotations, then scroll, sort and filter.
  5. Click an error in the list to bring its cell into view.
  6. Toggle the severity filter checkboxes to hide a severity's tints without changing the store.

Scenarios

Each scenario clears existing annotations first so they don't accumulate. Clear all empties the store directly.

Scale

90% on cells, 9% on rows and 1% on columns, in one addMany. Each click adds 100,000 more.

Navigate

actions.previousAnnotation() and nextAnnotation(), as Shift + F8 and F8; the checkbox calls showAnnotatedRows().
