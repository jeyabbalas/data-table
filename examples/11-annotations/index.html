About

table.annotations is a programmatic row / column / cell overlay. Phase 4 layers the visible rendering on top: CSS tints on rows / cells / column headers and a library-owned popover grouped by scope.

  1. Click a Scenario button (A–E) to seed a specific overlay.
  2. Hover or keyboard-focus an annotated cell or header to open the popover.
  3. Toggle the severity filter checkboxes to hide a severity tint without modifying the store.
