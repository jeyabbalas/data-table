Examples

Fourteen single-feature examples of @jeyabbalas/data-table. Each imports the package the way an app would, and keeps to one idea, small enough to copy.

  1. 01 Minimal The smallest mount: eight rows defined inline in the script, no network. createDataTableloadData
  2. 02 Load from URL 100,000 NYC taxi trips fetched as Parquet, with a live progress bar. loadProgressloadComplete
  3. 03 Programmatic filters Card, fare, tip and distance filters from buttons, each change logged. addFilteraddRawSQLFilter
  4. 04 Derived columns An expression column, a vector column, and a replacement that checks its dependents. addDerivedColumnreplaceDerivedColumn
  5. 05 Event listeners A side panel that observes the table with on() and drives it with actions. onoff
  6. 06 Custom theme A terracotta and cream palette, light and dark, all from CSS variables. --dt-*setColorScheme
  7. 07 i18n (French) Every label in French from one messages object, on a French wine list. messages
  8. 08 Custom visualization A US-states choropleth in place of the value counts on a state column. BaseVisualizationVisualizationRegistry
  9. 09 Multi-table Two tables sharing a worker, a session store and filter presets: save in A, load in B. FilterPresetManagerSessionStore
  10. 10 Column export Read a column out as a typed array, by the table's filters, sort and selection. getColumnValues__rowid__
  11. 11 Annotations Errors, warnings and info on rows, columns and cells, saved with the session. table.annotations
  12. 12 Column header tooltips Structured popovers on the column names: title, description and items. setColumnHeaderTooltip
  13. 13 Custom stats panel The column-stats slot replaced: mean and spread from a DuckDB query of its own. BaseStatsPanelStatsPanelRegistry
  14. 14 Standalone SQL editor CodeMirror editors outside the table that know its columns and DuckDB's functions. createSqlExtensionsbuildCompletionContext
